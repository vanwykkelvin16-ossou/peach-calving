    Who We Are

    Peach Capital South Africa is a private investment and consultancy firm dedicated to transforming the landscape for small and medium-sized enterprises.

    Our expertise lies in the acquisition, growth and consultancy of SMEs, giving business owners access to the same level of strategic insight, capital and operational support that large corporates enjoy. We bridge the gap between ambition and execution, ensuring every business we invest in or acquire thrives under our leadership.

    What sets us apart is our hands-on approach. We don't just invest; we collaborate. Our team brings sector knowledge and operating experience, guiding owners through key transitions — from scaling operations to optimising financial performance. Whether it's unlocking new revenue, refining strategy or preparing for a successful exit, we stand alongside our partners at every step.

    Peach Capital South Africa is a private company incorporated in South Africa and shares its ownership with Peach Capital in the United Kingdom. That means South African businesses get a local team with local judgement, backed by a group that has already built and exited SMEs offshore.

    What we stand for

    Championing the SME market

    SMEs employ most of South Africa's workforce, yet they are the most underserved by traditional finance. Peach Capital was built for their needs, not adapted from a corporate playbook. From funding to consultancy, we tailor our approach to founders and management teams who want to grow, compete and lead.

    Relationships first, always

    Our approach is collaborative, transparent and grounded in trust. We are approachable partners — honest about what we see, invested in the outcome, and clear in how we communicate. Strong relationships produce stronger results, so we build every partnership on respect and straight talk.

    Operators, not spectators

    Our team has run businesses, closed deals and signed audits. That depth means our partners get insight-driven strategy, informed decisions and a steady hand through every stage of growth — with people who have already done the work they're advising on.

    Who we help

    We work with owners who are ambitious and capable but don't have access to the resources typically reserved for larger companies. Whether you want to grow, prepare for a sale or navigate a major change, we provide the guidance and capital to move to the next level.

    We partner with management teams ready to expand but needing strategic support to unlock the opportunity — refining operations, entering new markets, professionalising finance and reporting.

    And we work with owners planning an exit or a handover. Whether that's preparing a business for sale, optimising its value or ensuring a smooth transition, we help owners realise the full value of what they've built.

    Our Approach

    Our approach is all about partnership. We work hand-in-hand with SME owners to understand their challenges and their ambitions.

    Whether we're making an investment, acquiring a business or stepping into a consultancy role, we immerse ourselves in the business and tailor our solutions to what that company actually needs. This ensures we don't just offer capital, but real strategic value.

    Above all, we prioritise transparency and clear communication at every stage. Optimising processes, navigating an acquisition, preparing for an exit — we are there throughout, making sure owners are equipped to make informed decisions.

    The six phases of an engagement

    1

    Identifying opportunities

    We work with owners to pinpoint where the potential sits — operational efficiency, new markets, or positioning the business for a successful sale. Assessing current strengths and gaps tells us where value can be created.

    2

    Due diligence and evaluation

    We review financials, operations and market position to build an accurate picture of the company's worth. This is also where we identify what must be fixed to make a business sale-ready and attractive to buyers.

    3

    Deal structuring

    We tailor the terms to align with both the value of the business and the goals of the owner — pricing, payment structure, warranties and exit path. Structure is where an ordinary deal becomes a good one.

    4

    Transition and integration

    We put processes in place for a seamless handover, preserving the value and culture of the business. For companies continuing to grow, we integrate new structures and reporting without disrupting the core.

    5

    Growth and optimisation

    Refining operations, expanding market presence, streamlining internal processes. Whether the goal is a higher sale price or long-term profitability, we work alongside management to deliver tangible results.

    6

    Exit and value realisation

    We make sure the business is ready for market and secure the best available outcome. By managing the exit deliberately, owners realise the full value of what they've built and move on to the next chapter.

    Why it works

    Unlike firms that simply provide capital, we integrate into each business we back — strategic insight, operational support and hands-on guidance. That level of involvement means every business gets the attention it needs, whether it's early in a growth run or preparing for sale.

    Our sector experience matters too. Deep operating knowledge lets us anticipate market shifts, fix operations and structure deals built for the long term.

    We don't chase one-time results. We focus on lasting value, which is why our involvement continues well after a transaction closes.

    What this looks like in South Africa

    Operating conditions here are their own discipline. Energy security, water reliability, logistics and municipal risk are real line items in a South African business plan, and we treat them as such — resilience planning, working capital design and supplier diversification are part of the value we add, not an afterthought.

    Ownership structure is also commercial. B-BBEE standing directly affects an SME's ability to hold and win corporate and public sector contracts, so we consider it in every transaction rather than treating it as a compliance exercise.

    How we assess a business

    Step 01

    Fair market value

    We determine fair market value using a blend of methods — EBITDA multiples, discounted cash flow and asset-based valuation. A balanced, data-driven view that reflects both current performance and future potential.

    Step 02

    Reputation and brand

    Reputation is a real asset. We assess brand strength, customer loyalty and market perception to gauge long-term viability and fit with how we invest.

    Step 03

    Quick to transact

    Our processes and team let us move swiftly from first conversation to close, minimising disruption to the business and giving owners certainty rather than a drawn-out process.

    Step 04

    A capable operating team

    We prioritise businesses with strong management. Their expertise and leadership are what sustain performance and drive growth after the transaction.

    Step 05

    Industry experience brought in

    Our network of sector veterans provides strategic insight and mentorship, lifting operational efficiency and bringing fresh thinking into portfolio companies.

    Step 06

    Balance sheet and EBIT multiple

    We analyse balance sheet health and apply appropriate EBIT multiples to arrive at a fair, defensible valuation that both sides can stand behind.

    What to expect, and when

      Investment Opportunities

      We focus on high-potential South African SMEs — businesses that are profitable, proven and ready for their next chapter.

      These companies sit beneath the radar of traditional private equity but offer significant upside when paired with strategic guidance and operational expertise. Our sweet spot is established firms with strong fundamentals that benefit from better structure, funding and focus.

      Deal flow is sourced through trusted networks, direct outreach and referrals, giving us access to opportunities that are frequently off-market. We take a disciplined, data-led approach to selection — assessing not just financials, but the people, the potential and the positioning of each business.

      For investors, that means a curated set of businesses across sectors, each with a tailored growth plan and a defined exit path, actively managed rather than left to run.

      Why now, and why South Africa

      The succession wave

      A large cohort of South African SME owners is reaching retirement, and many are emigrating or have no successor in place. These are profitable, well-established businesses without a clear plan for what happens next. We provide continuity — guiding owners through a clean exit while preserving the business and its jobs.

      Capital access is the constraint

      South African SMEs are chronically underfunded. Bank lending is collateral-driven and slow, and equity below R50m is poorly served. We bridge that gap with flexible capital and the strategic support to put it to work — which is precisely where the return sits.

      Operational and technology upgrades

      Many good South African businesses run on old systems, manual processes and fragile energy or logistics assumptions. Modernising operations is a repeatable, measurable source of margin — and it is exactly what our team does.

      A trusted approach

      Whether we're making an investment or stepping into a consultancy role, we immerse ourselves in the business and tailor our solutions to what it needs. Collaboration and trust are the foundation, and transparency runs through every stage of an engagement.
